Financing

Simple, transparent auto financing. All rates and payments below are demo figures in Kenyan shillings (KSh).

Demo data. Nothing on this page is an offer of credit. Rates, terms, and payments are illustrative and would not be honored. Amounts are shown in KSh.

How financing works

  1. Estimate your budget. Use the sample payment table below.
  2. Get pre-approved. (Not available on this demo — no backend.)
  3. Pick your vehicle. Amount financed = sale price − down payment − trade-in.
  4. Sign and drive. Final paperwork is signed at the dealership.

What affects your rate

  • Credit score and history
  • Loan term
  • New vs. used vehicle and vehicle age
  • Down payment amount
  • Debt-to-income ratio
  • Lender-specific policies

Documents to bring

  • Valid driver's license
  • National ID or passport
  • Proof of income
  • Proof of residence
  • Proof of insurance
  • Down payment or trade-in information
