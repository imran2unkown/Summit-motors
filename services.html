Frequently asked questions

Do I need to make an appointment for a test drive?

Yes — booking a slot helps us have the car cleaned, charged or fueled, and pulled up front. Walk-ins are welcome but may wait.

Can I trade in a car that isn't paid off?

Yes. We'll pay off the existing loan directly and apply any equity toward your purchase.

Do you offer financing for bad credit?

We work with lenders who serve a range of credit profiles. Bring proof of income and a down payment.

Do you deliver out of state?

Yes — within 100 miles free, longer-distance shipping for a fee. Airport pickup by appointment.

Is there a warranty on used vehicles?

Many vehicles carry the remainder of the factory warranty. Others are sold with a limited dealership warranty.

Can I return a car after buying it?

We offer a 3-day / 150-mile exchange policy on most vehicles. Ask your salesperson for specifics.
